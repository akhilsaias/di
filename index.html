Frequently Asked Questions

Insights on realism, lifespans, and platform safety.

What happens if work keeps me away for several days?

DiPet implements non-punitive gradual progression[cite: 1]. Missing a couple of meals causes mild sadness and hunger, but won't trigger critical illness[cite: 1]. Prolonged inactivity (up to 90 days) safely disables the pet, ready to be revitalized anytime[cite: 1]. Pets never die.[cite: 1]

How realistic are pet reactions and vocalizations?

Pets incorporate organic breathing, sniffing motions, dynamic tail wagging based on emotional arousal, and breed-accurate audio cues (barks, whines, purrs)[cite: 1].
